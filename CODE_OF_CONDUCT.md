# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, specific, patient, and considerate. Respect privacy and differing viewpoints. Focus criticism on ideas and code. Accept responsibility and repair harm when mistakes occur.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method published on the eventual repository. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project.
